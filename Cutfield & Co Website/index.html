Questions & Answers

Frequently asked questions

Who is Harrison Cutfield?
Harrison Cutfield is a Ray White real estate agent based on Auckland's North Shore, part of the Ray White Mairangi Bay and Milford office. He specialises in marketing and selling premium and luxury homes across Milford, Mairangi Bay, Forrest Hill, Campbells Bay, Browns Bay and the wider North Shore.
Which areas does Harrison cover?
Harrison sells right across the North Shore of Auckland. That spans Devonport, Takapuna, Milford, Forrest Hill and Sunnynook, the East Coast Bays (Castor Bay, Campbells Bay, Mairangi Bay, Browns Bay, Rothesay Bay, Torbay and Long Bay), and the western and northern suburbs including Northcote, Birkenhead, Glenfield, Albany and Greenhithe.
How do I get a free property appraisal?
Request a free, no-obligation appraisal through the form on this site, or call Harrison on 021 136 1955. He reviews recent comparable sales, comes prepared with tailored and up to date insights for your home, and will be in touch within one business day.
What are off-market listings and how do I see them?
Off-market homes are sold privately and are never advertised on TradeMe, realestate.co.nz or other marketplaces. Harrison keeps a private list of off-market North Shore homes. Unlock the current selection by entering your details on the Off-Market page.
How do I sell my house with Harrison?
Start with a free appraisal. Harrison then prepares a tailored marketing campaign, presents the home at its best, recommends the right sales method such as auction, deadline sale or by negotiation, and manages the whole process from the first conversation to the final signature.
Why sell with Harrison Cutfield and Ray White?
Harrison combines bespoke marketing, clear communication and sharp negotiation with the reach and reputation of the Ray White network, New Zealand's largest real estate group. Every campaign is tailored to the individual home, with refined presentation and honest advice.
