Real Estate in Takapuna

Selling in Takapuna? Harrison Cutfield is a Ray White North Shore agent with deep knowledge of the Takapuna market, offering free appraisals and tailored luxury marketing.

Takapuna is the North Shore's premier beachside town centre, where a white-sand swimming beach looks straight across to Rangitoto and the Hurstmere Road strip delivers some of Auckland's best dining only minutes from the water. Framed by the beach on one side and Lake Pupuke on the other, it is a suburb that genuinely lives up to its reputation.

The market spans prestige clifftop and beachfront homes, established villas on the leafy streets behind the centre, and a deep, active apartment market for buyers who want lock-and-leave living by the sea. Few North Shore suburbs attract such a wide and motivated buyer pool, from local upsizers to offshore and out-of-town purchasers.

For sellers, Takapuna rewards considered presentation and marketing that reaches beyond the local audience. A campaign built around the lifestyle, not just the floor plan, is what turns interest into strong, competing offers.
