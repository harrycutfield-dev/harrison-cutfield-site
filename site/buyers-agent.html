How it works with me

You brief me once. I run everything from there.

01
You authorise me

Tick the box below and I am your representative. From that point, every enquiry on a property goes through me, so your name and number stop landing on every listing agent's call list.

02
I enquire for you

Each home you are interested in, I contact the listing agent, get the full file (title, LIM, builders report if one exists, price feedback, offer deadlines) and book the viewing.

03
We view, then I dig

I walk through with you, or ahead of you if you are time poor. Then the due diligence: comparable sales, council records, zoning, what it really sold for last time.

04
I negotiate

Offer strategy, conditions, timing, and the conversation with the other side. You decide the number. I get it across the line.

Why buyers do this

One point of contact, and a professional on your side of the table.

No cold calls from listing agents

Your details stay with me. Agents get a buyers agent on the phone, not a buyer to work on.

Real pricing evidence

Someone to say no
